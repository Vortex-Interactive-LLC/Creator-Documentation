## Summary

A `WaterRegion` is the box of water that the `Water` option in Studio's Part dropdown adds. Everything inside it is under water: players swim, loose parts float, and the camera gets an underwater view below the surface. It is a [`Part`](./part.md), so it can be moved, rotated, and resized like one, and its top face is the surface. See the [Water guide](../../guides/water.md).

The region itself is invisible and cannot be collided with, touched, or hit by a raycast.
