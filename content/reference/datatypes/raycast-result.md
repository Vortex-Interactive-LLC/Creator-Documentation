## Properties

### Distance

> `number`
>
> The distance between the ray origin and the intersection point.

<br/>

### Instance

> `BasePart`
>
> The `BasePart` that the ray intersected.

<br/>

### Position

> `Vector3`
>
> The point at which the intersection occurred.

<br/>

### Material

> `Enum.Material`
>
> The `Enum.Material` at the intersection point.

<br/>

### Normal

> `Vector3`
>
> The normal face of the intersected face.
