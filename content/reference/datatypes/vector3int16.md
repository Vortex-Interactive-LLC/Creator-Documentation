## Summary

A `Vector3int16` is a position made of three whole numbers. It names a terrain cell in [`Terrain:PasteRegion`](../classes/terrain.md).

## Constructors

- `Vector3int16.new(x, y, z)` - any missing value is `0`.

## Properties

- `X`, `Y`, `Z` - `number`, the whole numbers.

Two values can be added, subtracted and negated, and compared with `==`.
