title: Accessories
description: Make and sell hats, hair, and other avatar accessories.
---
Accessories are 3D items that avatars wear, such as hats, hair, and backpacks. You can make them in any 3D tool and sell them in the catalog.

## Accessory types

Choose one type when you upload. Players can wear up to three hats and one of every other type.

| Type | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| Hat | On the head |
| Hair | On the head |
| Face | On the face |
| Neck | Around the neck |
| Shoulder | On the shoulders |
| Front | On the chest |
| Back | On the back |
| Waist | Around the waist |

## Making a model

Download the avatar template from the upload page and model your accessory around it. The accessory must stay in the same position and size as the template, because Vortex does not move it when it is worn. The avatar stands with its feet at the origin.

- A single `.glb` file, at most 2 MB;
- At most 2,000 triangles;
- One texture embedded in the file, PNG or JPEG, between 16 and 1024 pixels on each side;
- Static models only. Rigged or animated models are rejected;
- No glTF extensions, and no external files;
- No larger than 5 units in any direction, and close to the avatar.

Vortex rebuilds your file when you upload it, so only the geometry, the texture, and basic material settings are kept.

## Uploading

Upload on [Vortex Create](https://create.playvortex.io/clothing) with the Accessory option.

- Uploading costs 300 Volts. The fee is not refunded if the accessory is rejected;
- The lowest price is 50 Volts and the highest is 1,000,000 Volts;
- You receive 80% of each sale;
- You can upload up to 10 accessories an hour and 30 a day.

Every accessory is reviewed before it goes on sale. You can follow its status on the Items page, and if it is rejected, the reason is shown there.

## Archiving and deleting

Archive an accessory to take it off sale and move it out of your active items. Players who already own it keep it, and you can restore it from the Archived section on the Items page. A restored accessory stays off sale until you turn sale back on.

You can delete an accessory that no one else owns. Once another player owns it, you can only archive it. Deleting does not refund the upload fee.
